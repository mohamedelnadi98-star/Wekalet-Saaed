# Narrative cast portraits

Created for Saeed Agency v9 with the built-in image generation tool. Fictional characters, not photographs of identified real people. Original generated PNGs retained without pixel editing. Used locally by ui-story.js and embedded by the portable build.

Common prompt: photorealistic-natural; one square centered head-and-shoulders fictional Egyptian adult portrait; natural skin texture, cinematic window light, warm rim light, muted petrol-teal blurred warehouse background; no writing, logos, frames, collage or cartoon style.

- `saeed.png`: An Egyptian man aged 67, deceased founder seen in an old family portrait, warm lined face, salt-and-pepper mustache, balding silver hair, cream cotton shirt, dark olive sleeveless vest, kind thoughtful smile. Subtle warm archival color, not damaged.
- `rabie.png`: An Egyptian veteran delivery driver aged 58, sun-weathered brown skin, short gray hair, thick gray mustache, sturdy build, faded slate blue work shirt, grounded reassuring expression.
- `mona.png`: An Egyptian female grocery shop owner aged 36, oval face, warm medium olive skin, dark brown eyes, neatly wrapped muted sage hijab, sand-colored practical overshirt, attentive confident expression.
- `hossam.png`: An Egyptian dairy agency representative aged 33, slim angular face, clean-shaven, tidy short black hair, light blue button-up shirt, navy jacket, businesslike attentive expression.
- `sherif.png`: An Egyptian competing food distributor aged 44, broad face, close-cropped dark beard, slick short black hair, burgundy polo shirt, self-assured measured half smile, NOT villainous.
